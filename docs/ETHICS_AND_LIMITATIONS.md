# Ethics and Limitations

Gender in this project is **perceived gender inferred from first names**. It is probabilistic,
binary in the primary comparison, error-prone, and says nothing about a person's gender identity.
Names can be ambiguous, culturally variable, abbreviated, or misread by the lookup library.

Results are for aggregate comparisons only. We do not make individual-level claims about speakers,
companies, or intent. Salted speaker IDs keep names out of result-facing tables and dashboard
outputs. Unknown and confidence categories are retained, and manual audit tooling is provided.

The transcripts are observational. Associations cannot establish causation, bias, or the absence
of bias. AQS is a proxy rubric and should not be treated as ground truth. Small cells, missing
affiliations, transcript selection, and Q&A-marker failures can affect estimates.

Misuse risks include ranking individual speakers, treating inferred gender as identity, presenting
descriptive associations as discrimination findings, and over-interpreting small samples. To
reduce harm, the project uses salted IDs, reports uncertainty and coverage, separates silver from
gold labels, preregisters hypotheses, applies clustering and multiple-testing plans, and states
limitations in the dashboard.

Current fixture evidence is deliberately limited: 12 calls, 11 Q&A calls, and 12 exchanges. The
current summary reports no statistically tested primary results.
